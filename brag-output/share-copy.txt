Sahaay captions a lecture live, translates every line into 22 Indian languages with the technical terms kept intact, and explains the jargon as it is spoken, all on the laptop with the network switched off. On a CPU, the glossary model pushes Whisper past real time (RTF 0.41 to 1.55); on the Snapdragon Hexagon NPU, its encoder runs in 13.47 ms. Try it in your browser: sahaay-offline.vercel.app
